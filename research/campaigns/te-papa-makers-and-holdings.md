# Te Papa: named Rapanui makers and wider holdings

**Status: deferred thematic follow-up.** Te Papa's systematic catalogue
coverage is a programme priority; this brief preserves a focused maker-led
question and its known record locators.

## Question and rationale

Which documented works by named Rapanui makers are missing from existing MoSA
records? A focused maker-led pass broadens the collection's historical range
and prevents growth from treating Rapanui creativity only as anonymous old
material. It complements, and does not replace, broad geographic and material
discovery in the catalogue coverage campaign.

Use the existing [Te Papa catalogue recipe](../../.agents/skills/identify-objects/references/holder-lookups.md#the-metropolitan-museum-of-art-and-te-papa)
and [FE010655 source](../../collection/sources/te-papa-fe010655.json) as a
positive control. Existing institutional work is tracked under
[IndiGen](indigen-follow-up.md); this campaign owns discovery beyond that seed.

## Scope and search plan

Plan dated 30 September 2026. Inspect the public catalogue's
[Bene Aukara Tuki Pate page](https://collections.tepapa.govt.nz/agent/10876)
and all linked physical works, plus exact-name searches for that name and the
catalogue's displayed variants. Follow all result pages and record the count
and boundary. Explicit production attribution must come from an object record;
appearance under a related-person search is not enough.

Search the maker page's linked physical works and exact-name variants
systematically, following all relevant result pages. Explicit production
attribution must come from an object record; appearance under a related-person
search is not enough. Compare cultural affiliation, production place and
collection/find location rather than collapsing them. Include records without
images. Reconcile matches against all existing MoSA records and use the broad
catalogue campaign's results to avoid duplicating geographic and material
searches. Distinguish physical objects from natural-history specimens and mere
photographic depictions while retaining useful identification evidence.

## Reconnaissance and candidate position

On 30 September 2026, an official-domain web search for `Rapa Nui` and `Bene`
surfaced the [paoa record FE010656](https://collections.tepapa.govt.nz/object/268161).
Its detail page was readable and explicitly names the maker and 1996 production.
That registration number was absent from the collection's text search; it is a
likely addition requiring whole-collection identity comparison. The photograph
is marked All Rights Reserved, so this is not a cleared-image opportunity.
The maker page was also readable; its entire related-work set was not reviewed.
No new source or object was created during planning.

## Next action and completion

The 30 September 2026 reconnaissance surfaced the readable FE010656 record,
which explicitly names the maker and 1996 production, and the maker page; the
linked-work set was not reviewed. Reconcile FE010656 against existing paoa and
Te Papa records, then review the complete linked-work set and relevant name
variants. Preserve and integrate supported evidence through the ordinary
source workflow. Keep object type, replica status, making date and acquisition
date distinct. Do not infer a maker from accession adjacency or a general
collection story. Name and credit makers faithfully; foregrounding choices
with collaborators remain a separate editorial activity.
