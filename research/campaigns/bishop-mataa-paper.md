# Bishop Museum mata‘a paper

## Question and scope

Can specimens described by the [Mulrooney et al. paper](../../collection/sources/mulrooney-2014-bishop-mataa.json)
be matched with Bishop Museum collection records? The first pass transcribed
the appendix labels and compared them with MoSA's existing records. The paper's
citation, review findings and limitations belong in its source record. No
museum-wide census or automatic object creation is implied.

## Current position and unresolved work

The [working identifier list](../evidence/bishop-mataa-paper/sample-identifiers.txt)
contains labels and page locators, not a second source record or catalogue. It
is shared to avoid repeating transcription; the rest of the list remains
unreconciled.

The paper source currently links three object records—`bishop-mataa-c8353`,
`bishop-mataa-b3481c` and `bishop-mataa-b3481bbbb`—through ten attributed
claims. This is the coverage present in the collection; it does not mean all
paper samples were reconciled.

A 30 September 2026 comparison of the specimen labels and institution-scoped
variants with tracked collection JSON found no pre-existing identifier match.
Other Bishop-linked source claims concerned different artefact types. Earlier
exact-label web-index searches returned no indexed detail pages.

The supplied PDF, page images and raw OCR remain private in ignored
`research-local/bishop-mataa-paper/`. No PDF copy is included in Git or the
website. The supplied paper was reviewed on 30 September 2026 after direct
publisher downloads returned 404; the supplied copy's retrieval date is
unknown.

On 30 September 2026, exact Artifact Number searches in the [Bishop Museum
Ethnology Database](https://data.bishopmuseum.org/ethnologydb/entire.php)
returned zero records for `C8353`, `B3481c` and `B3481bbbb`, with every other
search field blank. These bounded form outcomes do not show that the objects
are absent or not held by the museum. The B3481 labels remain paper sample
numbers, not independently verified museum accessions. Search responses were
operational captures and are not retained.

## Next action

Investigate the accession conventions behind the paper's specimen labels using
a published Bishop inventory or collection documentation. Start with C8353,
which the paper explicitly calls an accession number, then establish whether
the B3481 sample suffixes correspond to institutional object numbers. Do not
repeat those exact searches without new evidence or a catalogue change. Keep
the paper's sample labels distinct from confirmed museum accessions. The three
linked specimen records represent the evidence currently reconciled;
catalogue-label research does not verify custody. A fuller review of claims and
images for the three objects remains unfinished. Do not repeat the PDF
transcription.
