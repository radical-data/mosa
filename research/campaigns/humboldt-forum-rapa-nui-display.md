# Humboldt Forum Rapa Nui display catalogue

## Question and scope

Which Rapa Nui objects from the Ethnologisches Museum are publicly displayed
at the Humboldt Forum, and how should their current exhibition records enrich
MoSA? This campaign covers the complete result set returned by the Humboldt
Forum Collections Online object catalogue for `Osterinsel` on 4 October 2026.
The catalogue states that it publishes the objects displayed at the Humboldt
Forum, so each result supplies current exhibition evidence as well as an
institution-scoped identifier.

The pass is complete when every result has an identity disposition, a preserved
or explicitly blocked detail source, reviewed claims and an image-rights
outcome. Existing MoSA objects receive the Humboldt Forum page as another
source. New objects are created only from verified catalogue records. Similar
unaccessioned entries from *Arte en la cultura rapanui* remain separate unless
object-specific evidence establishes a match.

## Search plan and coverage

On 4 October 2026 the English catalogue search for `Osterinsel` returned 31
records. All load-more results were expanded and every detail page was reviewed
through the final entry. The interface search for `Rapa Nui` returned no results,
so `Osterinsel` is the effective place-name route for this catalogue. Exact
accessions were compared with all MoSA `catalogue_number` claims rather than
matching on translated or generic classifications.

The bounded source is the [Humboldt Forum Collections Online result
set](https://sammlungenonline.humboldtforum.org/en/object-catalogue?query=Osterinsel).
This campaign does not claim broader coverage of the Ethnologisches Museum's
stored Rapa Nui holdings or of records omitted from the public-display
catalogue.

## Candidate outcomes

Ten entries match established MoSA objects by their displayed
institution-scoped accession: VI 12648, VI 12650, VI 13229, VI 2129, VI 42601,
VI 4864, VI 4868, VI 4901, VI 4913 and VI 4981. Their Humboldt Forum records are
additional sources, not new objects.

Twenty-one entries have accessions absent from MoSA and are supported as new
objects: VI 810; VI 4865; VI 4871 b; VI 4875; VI 4877 a; VI 4877 b; VI 4886;
VI 4888 <2>; VI 4890 a,b; VI 4892; VI 4894 a; VI 4919; VI 4924; VI 4930;
VI 4943; VI 4969; VI 4979; VI 4986; VI 54300; VI 54301; and VI 7240.

These 31 records describe displaced material attributed to Rapa Nui and managed
by the Ethnologisches Museum. They are artefact evidence in MoSA's scope. The
catalogue's combined place/culture field is retained as a source description,
not silently converted into a manufacturing-place claim.

## Unresolved work

The nine unaccessioned Berlin entries inherited from *Arte en la cultura
rapanui* remain unresolved against the modern catalogue. A shared type or
institution is insufficient to merge them with an accessioned object. Any
future crosswalk needs dimensions, provenance, an historical inventory number
or another object-specific identifier.

## Next action

The scoped integration is complete. All 31 catalogue records have preserved
HTML captures and attributed claims. The 21 new objects each have a separate
photograph source and an unchanged local image under the displayed CC BY-NC-SA
terms. The ten established objects link to the Humboldt Forum record as an
additional source; visual comparison also established that every displayed
Humboldt Forum photograph reproduces its existing SMB photograph source.

There are no remaining capture or image blockers in this result set. Resume
only when object-specific evidence can test the nine unaccessioned Berlin
entries inherited from *Arte en la cultura rapanui* against accessioned records.
