# Arte en la cultura rapanui: Vienna catalogue leads

The date above uses the 29 September 2026 legacy museum-search audit, the earliest dated work found in the retained notes. The extraction and some searches may be older, but no earlier date is recorded. This campaign resumed on 30 September 2026.

## Question and scope

Can four *Arte en la cultura rapanui* entries attributed to Museum für Volkerkunde, Vienna, be linked to individual records in the current Weltmuseum Wien collection? This selective identity pass covers `ao-viena`, `anzuelo-piedra-viena`, `cara-piedra-gris-viena` and `falo-viena`.

The [table source](../../collection/sources/arte-en-la-cultura-rapanui.json) supplies the original entries. Findings and their limits belong to the examined sources:

- [Weltmuseum Wien catalogue search](../../collection/sources/weltmuseum-wien-osterinsel-search.json): candidate search across the four entries, with preserved HTML.
- [A Colonial Thing](../../collection/sources/weltmuseum-wien-a-colonial-thing.json): a published stone-hook lead; the original PDF could not be captured.

Neither source establishes an object match for these entries.
