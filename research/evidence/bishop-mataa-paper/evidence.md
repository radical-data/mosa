## Reviewed findings

- Printed p. 303 reports 332 analysed Bishop Museum Ethnology Collection
  mata‘a, 302 complete and 30 broken.
- Printed pp. 324–334 contain 332 `Sample No.` table rows. Each row reports
  pXRF data, source assignments from SVM and DFA, and a brief description.
- Page counts for printed pp. 324–334 are 29, 32, 29, 32, 30, 32, 29, 32,
  29, 32 and 26 rows, respectively; their sum is 332.
- Printed p. 304 gives acquisition counts of 232 + 81 + 20 = 333. The paper
  does not explain the difference from the 332 analysed items and appendix rows.
