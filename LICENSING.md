## Indigenous knowledge and other exceptions

Some Indigenous data or knowledge may carry a separate licence, a Local
Contexts label or notice, or another community protocol. Follow the conditions
stated with that material. The general repository licences do not replace
those conditions or the authority of the relevant people and communities.

Pre-existing intellectual property remains with its owners. Fonts, logos,
branding, source captures and third-party dependencies retain their stated
terms. In particular, the Noto Sans font files retain the [SIL Open Font
Licence](public/fonts/noto-sans-OFL.txt).

Confidential information is not public repository material and is not licensed
for publication or reuse. Private research belongs in the ignored
`research-local/` directory.

The collaboration agreement separately governs decisions among the project
parties about jointly created intellectual property. It does not add
restrictions to rights already granted to the public under the standard
licences above.
